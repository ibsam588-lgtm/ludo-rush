Ludo Rush brings quick, colorful board-game matches to your phone. Play classic Ludo or switch things up with Snakes & Ladders, then challenge friends, find an online rival, or practice against offline players.

PLAY YOUR WAY
- Jump into fast 2-player or 4-player Ludo matches
- Create a private room and share the code with friends
- Play offline when you want a relaxed solo match
- Climb ladders, dodge snakes, and race to the finish

BUILD YOUR COLLECTION
- Earn points by completing matches and opening rewards
- Unlock dice skins, board themes, and player avatars
- Preview cosmetics in the Shop before equipping them
- Watch optional reward videos for bonus points and energy

FAIR, FAMILIAR RULES
- Roll a six to bring a piece into play
- Land on an opponent to capture it and earn another turn
- Safe tiles protect pieces from capture
- Exact rolls are required to reach home
- Rolling a six also grants another turn

Whether you have a minute for a quick round or want to settle a rivalry with friends, Ludo Rush keeps the board ready.
