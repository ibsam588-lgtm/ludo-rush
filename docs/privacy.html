Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

Canonical policy URL: https://corsairlabs.com/ludo-cheer-privacy-policy

This policy explains how Ludo Cheer processes information when you play, use social features, view ads, or make a Google Play or Apple App Store purchase.

Information We Process

  • Guest player ID, display name, selected country or matchmaking region, avatar selection, and age setting.
  • Gameplay and social information such as match rooms, results, ratings, coins, rewards, friend relationships, clubs, reactions, and in-app messages.
  • When you choose to join table voice chat, microphone audio is transmitted to the other human players in that match. Connection details are exchanged through Cloudflare. The app does not record or store voice audio. Voice blocks and reports store player and room IDs, a report reason, time, and review status for safety enforcement.
  • Google Play purchase records, including product ID, purchase token, purchase status, and the entitlement or coins delivered. Payment card details are handled by Google Play and are not received by Ludo Cheer.
  • Apple App Store signed transaction identifiers, product and entitlement details, purchase status, and an app-account token derived from the guest player ID. Apple handles payment-card details.
  • On iOS, optional Game Center sign-in uses your player profile, authentication status, online-win leaderboard score, and achievement progress. The game remains playable without Game Center.
  • Technical and advertising information processed by Unity LevelPlay and its mediated ad networks, including IP-derived approximate location, advertising ID or other device identifiers, ad views and taps, app interaction data, and diagnostics.

How We Use Information

  • Run quick match, private rooms, bot matches, and reconnect-ready room state.
  • Connect consenting human players for optional live voice chat during matches; mute, block, report, and leave controls are available in the game.
  • Maintain match history, coins, rewards, and ratings.
  • Verify purchases, prevent duplicate grants, restore permanent purchases, and maintain the in-game economy.
  • Serve and measure ads, limit fraud, analyze ad performance, and maintain app and service reliability.

Ads And Purchases

Ludo Cheer uses Unity LevelPlay to serve banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. LevelPlay and participating ad networks may collect and share approximate location, purchase history, ad interactions, diagnostics, and device or advertising identifiers for advertising or marketing, analytics, app functionality, and fraud prevention, security, and compliance. Users can reset or delete the Android advertising ID in Android settings.

Optional digital items are sold through Google Play Billing on Android and Apple StoreKit on iOS. Google purchase tokens are sent securely to the backend and Google Play for verification and stored only as one-way hashes. Apple's signed transactions are verified before the backend grants coins or entitlements. The transaction ledger prevents duplicate delivery and supports restoring permanent purchases.

On iOS, App Tracking Transparency controls access to the advertising identifier and tracking where required. Declining tracking does not remove access to gameplay. Participating ad networks may include ironSource Ads, Unity Ads, Chartboost, InMobi, Liftoff Monetize, and Meta Audience Network; data use depends on your region, privacy choices, and the networks serving ads.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data is processed by service providers used to operate the app, including Cloudflare, Google Play on Android, and Apple App Store and Game Center on iOS. If you join voice, live audio is shared with the human players in that match and may be relayed by Cloudflare TURN when a direct connection is unavailable. Advertising data may be shared with Unity LevelPlay and the ad networks participating in its mediation service to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud.

Children

Ludo Cheer is intended for players age 13 and older and is not primarily directed to children. The app sends the corresponding child-directed setting to its advertising SDK before initialization.

Data Retention

Account, gameplay, social, and purchase-ledger data is retained while needed to operate the game, restore purchases, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations. Voice audio is not recorded or stored by the app; voice connection credentials expire. Advertising providers retain data under their own policies.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app owner through the GitHub repository: Ludo Cheer on GitHub.
