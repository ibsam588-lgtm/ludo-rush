Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 7, 2026

Ludo Rush is currently an internal testing game. This policy explains what information may be processed while testing the app.

Information We Process

  • Guest player ID and display name.
  • Gameplay information such as match rooms, results, ratings, coins, and connection state.
  • Basic technical information needed to operate multiplayer services, such as timestamps and region selection.

How We Use Information

  • Run quick match, private rooms, bot matches, and reconnect-ready room state.
  • Maintain match history, coins, rewards, and ratings.
  • Debug gameplay, networking, and service reliability during internal testing.

Ads And Purchases

The current internal test build includes code hooks for ads and in-app purchases, but production ad serving and paid purchases are not active until store credentials and provider integrations are configured.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data may be processed by infrastructure providers used to run the app, such as Cloudflare and Google Play services when configured.

Children

Ludo Rush is not intended for children under 13 during internal testing. Testers should be adults or have appropriate permission from a parent or guardian.

Data Retention

Internal testing data may be retained while the test is active and deleted or reset as development requires.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app owner through the GitHub repository: Ludo Rush on GitHub.
