Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 12, 2026

Canonical policy URL: https://corsairlabs.com/ludo-rush-privacy-policy

This policy explains how Ludo Rush processes information when you play, use social features, view ads, or make a Google Play purchase.

Information We Process

  • Guest player ID, display name, selected country or matchmaking region, avatar selection, and age setting.
  • Gameplay and social information such as match rooms, results, ratings, coins, rewards, friend relationships, clubs, reactions, and in-app messages.
  • Google Play purchase records, including product ID, purchase token, purchase status, and the entitlement or coins delivered. Payment card details are handled by Google Play and are not received by Ludo Rush.
  • Technical and advertising information processed by Unity LevelPlay and its mediated ad networks, including IP-derived approximate location, advertising ID or other device identifiers, ad views and taps, app interaction data, and diagnostics.

How We Use Information

  • Run quick match, private rooms, bot matches, and reconnect-ready room state.
  • Maintain match history, coins, rewards, and ratings.
  • Verify purchases, prevent duplicate grants, restore permanent purchases, and maintain the in-game economy.
  • Serve and measure ads, limit fraud, analyze ad performance, and maintain app and service reliability.

Ads And Purchases

Ludo Rush uses Unity LevelPlay to serve banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. LevelPlay and participating ad networks may collect and share approximate location, purchase history, ad interactions, diagnostics, and device or advertising identifiers for advertising or marketing, analytics, app functionality, and fraud prevention, security, and compliance. Users can reset or delete the Android advertising ID in Android settings.

Optional digital items are sold through Google Play Billing. Purchase tokens are sent securely to the Ludo Rush backend and Google Play for verification. Purchase tokens are stored only as one-way hashes in the Ludo Rush purchase ledger.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data is processed by service providers used to operate the app, including Cloudflare and Google Play. Advertising data may be shared with Unity LevelPlay and the ad networks participating in its mediation service to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud.

Children

Ludo Rush is intended for players age 13 and older and is not primarily directed to children. The app sends the corresponding child-directed setting to its advertising SDK before initialization.

Data Retention

Account, gameplay, social, and purchase-ledger data is retained while needed to operate the game, restore purchases, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations. Advertising providers retain data under their own policies.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app owner through the GitHub repository: Ludo Rush on GitHub.
